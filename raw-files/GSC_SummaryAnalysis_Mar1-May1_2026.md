## What this means for Stupski / CONTROL

CONTROL is the strongest organic asset the site has after the foundation's own brand name. Demand is overwhelmingly **author-led** ("glen galaich", "glen galaich book", "glen galaich control") and **subtitle-led** ("control why big giving falls short" in many variants). The book's landing page `/control/` ranks at position 3.1 with 14% CTR — a sign the page is well-titled and well-described. The companion blog post and book-tour page both rank well but have lower CTRs and could benefit from sharper meta descriptions and schema.

The big opportunities I would surface to Stupski:

The `/break-fake-rules/` page is generating an enormous impression footprint (1.2M) at 0% CTR and position ~10. That is almost certainly an unintended ranking on a generic phrase. Rewriting the title tag and meta description to lead with "Break Fake Rules — Glen Galaich / CONTROL" would convert a portion of that into book-aware traffic instead of bouncing it.

Mobile CTR is more than 2× desktop CTR but only carries 15% of impressions. Pushing CONTROL on social and via the podcast (which skews mobile) is a higher-yield channel than desktop SEO.

The "Break Fake Rules" podcast is gaining traction — 96 clicks for "break fake rules" plus 78 for "break fake rules podcast" at >50% CTR — and individual guest names (Jennifer Nguyen) are starting to rank. Episode-level pages with proper podcast/episode schema would compound this.

There is no rich-result presence at all. Adding Book schema to `/control/` (with author, ISBN, publisher, sample, reviews) and Event schema to `/control/book-tour-events/` is low-effort, high-value.

International demand is small but consistent in Canada/UK/Australia. If Stupski is doing any paid amplification of CONTROL, those three markets are warm.
