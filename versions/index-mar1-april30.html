Top Desktop Click Targets on /control/

Clarity

1,755 total clicks tracked — same modal close pattern, smaller volume

🚨
The promo modal is the #1 UX issue across both device categories: On mobile, 1,781 taps (41.5% of all interactions) went to closing the modal vs. just 33 taps (0.77%) on the modal's CTA button. On desktop, 454 clicks (25.9%) went to the modal close vs. 24 clicks (1.37%) on its CTA. Mobile users are even more aggressively dismissing the modal than desktop users. The modal is interrupting engagement, not driving it.
⚠️
JavaScript errors disproportionately affect iOS users: 465 sessions (41% of all JS errors) encountered the "window.webkit.messageHandlers" error, which is specific to iOS Safari and in-app browsers like Facebook's. Given that 43% of sessions came from MobileSafari and 30% from FacebookApp, this is impacting nearly half of all mobile visitors. Worth investigating with the dev team.
