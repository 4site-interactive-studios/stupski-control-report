Oct 23 – Nov 2, 2026, Google Ads Demand Gen
Google Ads became the largest source of /control/ traffic within ten days
$1,645 in Demand Gen spend bought 22,543 ad clicks and about 9,500 GA4 sessions, which is 61% of all /control/ landings since June 1. Visits were short (under 7 seconds of engagement on average), but they produced 75 book retailer clicks, 53 of them to Amazon. The new Google Ads tab has the full breakdown.
🧐
Sept 3, Oct 15 and Nov 2, 2026, Automated traffic
Three automated traffic bursts are excluded from these numbers
On Sept 3, Oct 15 and Nov 2, 5,053 visits arrived in short bursts of about an hour each, carrying garbled campaign tags. Nearly all were on desktop Chrome, and each one viewed a single page and left within seconds. That pattern points to software scanning links, most likely email security tools at the recipients’ organizations, not to people reading. These visits are removed from every GA4 figure on this page. Uncheck the switch above to see them.
How to fix: Ask SSIR for their delivered click counts for Sept 2 to 3 and Oct 15, and whether their links pass through a security or click-tracking service. Then build a GA4 segment that filters out these tags so future reports stay clean.

Where 22,543 Clicks Went

Google AdsGA4

Ad network for the fall Demand Gen campaign, and how many ad clicks GA4 could tie back to a session

🚨
Only about 42% of ad clicks became a GA4 session. Google Ads counted 22,543 clicks; GA4 recorded about 9,500 sessions from them. Some gap is normal (people tap and leave before the page loads, and consent or ad blockers stop the tag), but a gap this large on a campaign bidding for the most clicks usually means many accidental taps inside apps. Most visits came through in-app browsers on mobile, and the average visit lasted about 6 seconds.
How we have fixed: We’ve updated the live NewReach campaign, and the setup for all future campaigns, to optimize for engaged readers rather than cheap clicks. We reviewed the placements and "where ads showed" reports, excluded the apps and sites with the lowest engagement, and turned on a frequency cap. Once a retailer-click conversion is set up, current and future campaigns will move from Maximize clicks to Maximize conversions.

Stupski's UTM Convention

From the UTM link generator's Campaigns and Sources tab. All values lowercase, words joined with underscores, no spaces.

utm_campaign (required)
One of three: control_book_launch, break_fake_rules, stupski
utm_source (required)
Where the link lives: ssir, chronicle_philanthropy, inside_philanthropy, nonprofit_quarterly, linkedin, meta, google, stupski, qr_code
utm_medium (required)
From the Legend: display, email, social, paid_social, print, referral, cpc
utm_content / utm_term (optional)
Content: purchase, free_chapter, business_card, flyer. Term: dedicated_blast
💡
Why the convention matters in GA4: GA4 treats SSIR and ssir, or fall_campaign and fall campaign, as different values, and it sorts traffic into channels by medium. display, email, social and paid_social land in the right channel automatically; print traffic shows as Unassigned, so filter QR results by medium.
How to fix: Build every link in the UTM link generator rather than by hand, and keep the medium list to the defined values only.
