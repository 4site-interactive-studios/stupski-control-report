Is the Fall Campaign Traffic Human?

GA4Clarity

Fingerprint of the three largest fall_campaign sources, Aug 1 – Oct 6

🚨
Both large sources look automated. The scrambled-UTM burst and the SSIR banner come from the same cloud data-center cities (Moses Lake WA, San Jose CA, Des Moines IA, Ashburn VA, Phoenix AZ, Toronto, Canberra, Dublin, Amsterdam, Singapore), almost entirely desktop Chrome, with about one page per session. Clarity logged only 36 sessions referred by ssir.org from Sept 1 to Oct 6. The SSIR newsletter traffic (109 sessions, 2.07 pages per session, 69s average) looks human. Ask SSIR for their delivered click count before reporting the banner numbers.

Proposed UTM Convention for Stupski

GA4 treats Email and email as different values, so one lowercase vocabulary keeps reports clean

utm_source
Where the link lives: linkedin, ssir, chronicle-of-philanthropy, mailchimp, substack
utm_medium
One of: social, paid-social, email, display, cpc, qr, referral
utm_campaign
Initiative and season: control-book-fall-2026, bfr-podcast-2026
utm_content
Creative or placement: banner-300x250, post-2026-09-15

Brand search held up after the launch window: "stupski foundation" drew 3,410 Google clicks at a 67% CTR across the period, 1,632 of them since June 1. "glen galaich" and the Break Fake Rules podcast remain the next largest branded queries, all ranking between positions 1.0 and 1.9.

Core Web Vitals and behavioral insights for the scope selected above

Core Web Vitals
Behavioral Insights
💡
What Core Web Vitals are telling us: Google's thresholds for "good" are LCP (load time) under 2.5 seconds, INP (tap responsiveness) under 200ms and CLS (layout shift) under 0.1. Site-wide field data shows LCP at 5.5s and CLS at 0.34, so pages still load slowly and shift as they settle. /control/ improved a little since June (LCP 3.5s) but CLS stays poor at 0.35.
